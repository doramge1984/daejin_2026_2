DATA 210: Data and Society — Course Syllabus, Fall 2026

Westbrook University (fictional) · Department of Information Studies · 3 credits · Tue/Thu 10:00–11:15
Instructor: Dr. Jane Rivera (fictional) · Office hours: Wed 14:00–16:00 or by appointment

Course Description

Data shapes how we see the world, from news charts to AI recommendations. In this course, students learn to find, question, and communicate with data. There are no exams; instead, students build a portfolio of short data stories and one team project for a real community partner.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

  1. Locate and clean an open dataset for a stated purpose.
  2. Critique a published chart and redesign it to reduce distortion.
  3. Distinguish correlation from causation in news claims, citing evidence.
  4. Communicate a data-based recommendation to a non-expert audience in 5 minutes.
